## How to use this file

- **Anh tự đăng ký.** This guide does not sign up, submit forms, or accept terms for you. Read each program’s current terms yourself before ticking any box.
- **QUICK** = realistically possible to get a usable account (or live tracking links) **before Fri 27 Nov 2026**. **SLOW** = official review window, extra sales/content tests, or brand-by-brand approval make a pre-BF go-live unlikely. A QUICK network account still does not mean every advertiser on that network will approve you this week.
- **Commission** is quoted **only** when the official page stated a number. Otherwise it is `unknown`. “Up to X%” is the vendor’s own wording, not a guaranteed rate.
- **VN-resident eligible** is “Yes” only when the official page clearly serves Vietnam publishers / does not restrict the country. “Likely — confirm on the form” means no official ban was found, but the form or tax-residency list must be checked live. “Conditional” means extra constraints (followers, established site, etc.).
- Do **not** invent or paste coupon/cookie durations, treaty rates, or payout numbers from blogs. If a field is `unknown`, leave it until the official dashboard or terms show it.
- Advertising disclosure belongs on the deals site (and on social posts) wherever affiliate links appear. US programs (Amazon, Impact, PartnerStack) expect this; VN platforms also require accurate identity and no misleading claims.

## Prepare once, then reuse

Have these ready before opening the first form:

1. Live Cloudflare Pages URL (custom domain if you have one — stronger than a `*.pages.dev` URL).
2. Short site description: “Black Friday / Cyber Monday deals site for shoppers in Vietnam and internationally; editorial deal roundups with category and VN/intl filters.”
3. Traffic source: “Own website (new), plus any social channels you actually control.” Be honest that the site is new and has no traffic history. Lying gets accounts closed.
4. Vietnamese citizen ID (both sides, sharp, four corners, no glare) + personal tax ID. From 1 Jul 2025, Shopee’s official payment guide states the personal identification number officially replaces the tax ID for individuals / households; still upload the tax-lookup screenshot they ask for. Official lookup page: http://tracuunnt.gdt.gov.vn/tcnnt/mstcn.jsp
5. VN bank **account number** (not the domestic ATM card number), account name matching the ID, bank + branch / CITAD / SWIFT as requested.
6. International payout rails you actually have or can open: PayPal, Payoneer, and (if you use them) Stripe. Many US/EU networks pay these, not a VN bank directly.
7. For US-sourced commissions: you will usually complete an online **W-8BEN** (individual, non-US). This is a tax form, not legal advice. Amazon officially withholds up to 30% unless a valid treaty claim is on file. Check the current IRS treaty list yourself before claiming a reduced rate — this file does not state a Vietnam treaty rate.
8. Do **not** apply to Amazon Associates until the site has substantial original, recent public content (official review bar: a rule of thumb of at least 10 posts, content generally within the last 60 days). Applying too early can burn the application — rejected IDs cannot be reinstated.
